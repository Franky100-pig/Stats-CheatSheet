Statistics Cheat Sheet

A formula-first AP Statistics lecture, written in chapters. The chapters follow the AP Statistics Course and Exam Description, effective Fall 2026 — five units, 55 topics — in order; every sheet has a print-ready A4 PDF. Formulae, symbols and TI-Nspire command names stay in English — the language of the exam — while the Chinese annotations explain the reasoning, the conditions and the traps.

How this book is organised

One chapter per topic of the course framework. Under each topic you get the knowledge points in Chinese, so the whole syllabus map is visible before the notes exist; a topic that already has a sheet links straight to it, with the matching A4 PDF. Units that are not written yet are listed with what they will cover.

The rule for every chapter

  • Name the parameter first: p, μ, p1 − p2, μ1 − μ2 or μd.
  • Check the conditions (Random, 10%, Normal / Large Counts) and write them in words.
  • Run it on the calculator, then interpret it in context — never in isolation.

Contents

How to read a chapter

Each chapter opens with a Chinese reading guide, then runs section by section: the English text carries the exam language, and a short Chinese note under each heading explains what that section is really saying. A chapter ends with the matching TI-Nspire commands, so the derivation and the calculation sit on the same page.

The Quick Reference & Tools appendix holds the working pieces: the inference procedure picker, the notation table, the exam traps and the calculator companion.

Where new notes go

Every statistics note belongs here, AP Statistics first. A new idea that fits an existing chapter extends that sheet; a genuinely new topic becomes a new sheet with its own PDF, a row in the contents above and a matching Chinese gloss in tools/i18n_zh.py. The course outline itself — units, topics and their Chinese knowledge points — is written once in tools/chapters.py, following the Fall 2026 course and exam description.
